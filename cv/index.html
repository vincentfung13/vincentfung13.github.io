Experience

  • 2023 - present

    Singapore

    Staff Applied Scientist, Business Integrity
    TikTok Pte. Ltd.
    I lead a team of 7 full-time scientists and engineers building the AI foundation for TikTok Business Integrity, with a focus on large-scale foundation model data, moderation intelligence, and agentic system evaluation.
    • Agentic ads moderation system. Built an agentic content moderation system from scratch, end to end: problem definition, data curation, agentic SFT, agentic RL and serving. Adaptive feature selection through tool calls improves recognition performance and adapts quickly to policy changes, at a throughput of more than 2 QPS per NVIDIA H20.
    • Large-scale synthetic data for foundation models. Built a fine-grained integrity knowledge graph of more than 300 entities and an online multimodal synthetic-data pipeline that grounds real-world videos to granular risk concepts and generates aligned QA, captions, spatial grounding and agentic moderation tasks. The pipeline generates more than 2B tokens a day and continuously improves our in-house foundation model.
    • Agentic system evaluation. Built large-scale evaluation infrastructure and benchmark datasets for internal risk-operation agents, with automatic multi-round conversations against a user simulator, sandboxed trajectory rollouts with more than 20 internal skills, and evaluation of long-horizon tasks and memory.
